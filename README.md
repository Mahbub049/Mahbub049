## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am **Muhammad Mahbub Sarwar Shafi**, a **Lecturer, Researcher and Software Engineer** based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

My work sits at the intersection of **computer science education, artificial intelligence and software engineering**. I enjoy transforming research ideas and real institutional problems into practical software systems — from explainable machine-learning models to academic management platforms and modern full-stack applications.

Currently, I am a **Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at BUBT**, while continuing research in **Explainable AI, Natural Language Processing, Biomedical AI and Machine Learning**.
